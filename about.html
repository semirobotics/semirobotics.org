Company Overview

SemiRobotics Consulting Pte Ltd is a Singapore-based technical consulting firm specialising in vacuum transfer robot (VTR) systems used within semiconductor front-end process equipment. The company was founded by engineers with extensive field and development experience across atmospheric and sub-atmospheric robotic wafer-handling systems.

Our work spans both original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and end-user fabs, with a practical understanding of the distinct technical requirements, operational constraints, and quality standards that apply at each level of the semiconductor equipment value chain.

SemiRobotics Consulting operates at the intersection of mechanical engineering, motion systems, vacuum science, and semiconductor process knowledge — a combination that is rare, and that directly enables higher-quality technical outcomes.

Engineering Philosophy

We approach every robot system as an integrated assembly — not a collection of isolated components. Effective calibration cannot be separated from an understanding of the process environment. Effective troubleshooting requires knowledge of how mechanical tolerances, vacuum conditions, and control system behaviour interact over time.

Our methodology is structured, documented, and traceable. We work to OEM specifications where they exist, and apply rigorous engineering judgement where they do not.

Mission

To deliver the most technically rigorous, responsive vacuum transfer robot services available — supporting semiconductor manufacturers and equipment providers in maintaining system integrity, process repeatability, and maximum tool uptime.
